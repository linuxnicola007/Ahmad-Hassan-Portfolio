About

I'm a penetration tester and digital forensics analyst based in Pakistan, focused on offensive security testing and post-incident investigation. My work spans network and application penetration testing, enterprise red-teaming, and digital evidence analysis — most recently validated through EC-Council's two most advanced credentials: the Licensed Penetration Tester (Master) and the Certified Penetration Testing Professional (C|PENT), the latter scored at 94%.

Alongside that, I serve as Director Finance for InfoSec Alliance at PAF-IAST, and keep competing in CTFs — as xlawara — on picoCTF and TryHackMe.
